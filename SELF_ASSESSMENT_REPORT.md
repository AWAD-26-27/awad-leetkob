## What we did not manage

All deliverables within the scope of PA#2 (engineering harness, quality gate, publish contract and hardened tests) have been completed and verified. Disclosed technical trade-offs and future scope:
1. Plain JavaScript: No compile-time static type checker is configured (disclosed in Criterion 3 rather than simulating with an empty script).
2. Model Integration & Budget: Gemini key is provisioned with Google AI Studio Free tier (effective paid spending limit of USD 0, no billing account linked). Model discovery (`GET /v1beta/models`) was verified manually, but automated inference pipeline, prompt evaluation, and live judging are deferred to PA#3/PA#4 in accordance with assignment scope.
3. Live model calls in CI: In accordance with project rules, CI and automated tests do not call external LLM endpoints or require real secrets.
4. Deliberate break: PR #5 was properly closed without merging to keep `main` clean and reproducible.

## What we would do differently

Inspect assertions alongside each green CI result earlier: counting passing tests did not reveal that a caught assertion concealed an HTTP 200/409 mismatch. Record red/green runs and required-check evidence while each incident happens, and agree model budget and member identities before the final packaging step.
